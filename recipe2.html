Schezwan Maggi

Prep time: 5 mins   Cook time: 10 mins   Serves: 1-2

Ingredients

  • 2 packets Maggi noodles (+ tastemaker)
  • Schezwan sauce or chutney
  • 1 tsp of Turmeric powder
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 small capsicum
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Soy sauce
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Spring onion and coriander for garnish (optional)
  • Salt and Pepper as needed

Steps

  1. Chop the onion, capsicum, and garlic.
  2. Boil the Maggi noodles for about 2 minutes less than the packet says, then drain and keep aside.
  3. Mix the boiled maggie with turmeric powder, salt and a littble bit of oil.
  4. Heat oil in a pan, sauté garlic, onion, and capsicum for 2 minutes.
  5. Add the Schezwan sauce, soy sauce, and tastemaker in the pan and mix well.
  6. Add a cup of water after mixing it.
  7. Toss in the boiled noodles and mix until everything is coated and it becomes partially dry.
  8. Garnish with spring onion and coriander. Eat it before it gets cold - that's the hostel rule.

Backstory: My only relief in my last hostel's difficult days. My friend taught me this recipe. (Original recipe by Chef Debasis.)
