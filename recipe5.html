Paneer Butter Masala

Prep time: 10 mins   Cook time: 20 mins   Serves: 3-4

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer, cubed
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsp butter and 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 cup fresh cream
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Salt and a pinch of sugar to taste

Steps

  1. Heat butter and oil together, fry the onion until soft.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute.
  3. Add the chopped tomatoes and spices, cook until the oil separates.
  4. Add a little water and simmer for 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in the cream and kasuri methi, then add the paneer cubes and simmer 5 more minutes.
  6. Serve with naan or jeera rice.

Backstory: Another banger recipe from my mother. My favorite out of any other recipe. (Original recipe by Chef Mummy.)
